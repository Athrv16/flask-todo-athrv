About Daily Flow

A simple, focused task manager built for everyday productivity

What is Daily Flow?

Daily Flow is a lightweight, web-based task management application designed to help you organize your day without distractions. It lets you create, manage, and track tasks with optional timed reminders — all from a clean, distraction-free interface. Whether you're a student managing assignments, a professional tracking deadlines, or anyone who likes staying organized, Daily Flow keeps things simple and effective.

Why Daily Flow?

Most task apps are bloated with features you never use. Daily Flow was built with one philosophy: do less, better. No project boards, no Gantt charts, no team collaboration — just you and your to-do list. It was created as a full-stack learning project to explore Flask, MongoDB, and real-time browser notifications, and has since grown into a fully functional productivity tool.
